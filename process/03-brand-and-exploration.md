# 03 Brand and exploration

## One family, one red

The brand system is small on purpose. Every new screen in the app, the site and the coming soon page draws from the same few tokens.

- **Color:** Phorcast red `#E5331E` is the only brand color. Green and red mean buy and sell, gain and loss, and nothing else.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans for every role, chosen by the client from my tests. One body size: 16px, 400, 1.45.
- **Marks:** 102 market marks in the app. Every market wears its real logo, drawn to three rules: full size of the slot with no tile behind it, the real file from the project or club, and flags for indices and forex.

## 22 hero compositions, one shipped

For the coming soon page I went wide before narrow: five layouts, two rounds of background treatments, five blue-sky directions, then two syntheses. Texture never survived review. The gauge did.

| Direction | Round | Result | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="../img/explore/v2-split.jpg" alt="Split, live market" width="320"> Split, live market | Round two | Dropped | Hairline grids and a radial glow to add depth. Texture, not design power. |
| <img src="../img/explore/v3-contour.jpg" alt="Contour field" width="320"> Contour field | Round three | Dropped | Stronger backgrounds behind the same layout. The page got busier, not better. |
| <img src="../img/explore/v3-chart-field.jpg" alt="Chart field" width="320"> Chart field | Round three | Dropped | A chart behind the headline made the headline harder to read. |
| <img src="../img/explore/x4-gauge.jpg" alt="The gauge" width="320"> The gauge | Blue sky | Kept | A semicircular gauge at a market's chance of Yes. The four other blue-sky directions were deleted. |
| <img src="../img/explore/x6-conviction.jpg" alt="Conviction" width="320"> Conviction | Synthesis | Kept | Two editorial columns, a full 0 to 100 probability rule, a flat window. No shadows, glows or gradients. |
| <img src="../img/explore/x7-instrument.jpg" alt="Instrument" width="320"> Instrument | Synthesis | **Shipped** | The gauge with a calm chart band and a single-line headline, set in Instrument Sans. The client picked it. |
